## Tesla Roadster car model

`game/assets/car_chassis.glb` and `car_wheel.glb` are Draco-compressed conversions of the car from
[Jaagrav/Roadster](https://github.com/Jaagrav/Roadster) (`static/models/car/chassis.gltf` and
`wheel.gltf`). That repository has no license file and no stated model author, so the license of
this car is **unknown**. If you are the author or rights holder and want it removed, open an issue
and it will be taken down. If you reuse this repo, replace the car with your own.
